Actions, safely

Try “create a channel called roadmap”, “set slowmode in this channel to 30 seconds”, or “assign the Member role to @person”. You must already have the relevant Discord permission. AsterByte proposes only supported actions, then asks you to confirm. It checks current permissions, bot permissions, and role hierarchy again before acting. Confirmations expire after five minutes.

No unrestricted shell, remote browser, arbitrary MCP tools, role privilege escalation, or autonomous destructive actions. A prompt cannot grant permissions. Use /access for capabilities and /action history for recent action records.

Opt-in memory and automation

“Remember this conversation” enables your channel-scoped context for 24 hours. Responses remain visible to channel readers. “Forget this conversation” clears it. Disabling use alone does not delete saved context.

Server Pro managers can say “watch this channel and post a daily digest”. A public channel notice appears first; each run uses at most 100 recent human messages and the automation expires in 30 days. Say “show digests”, “run the digest now”, or “stop the digest”. This is periodic summarization, not continuous autonomous monitoring. Digests currently use the shared beta account, never personal connected keys.

Accounts, keys, and quota

Say “my connection” to see which account is used. Interactive AI requests use your connected key when present; failures do not silently switch to the operator's account. Without a connection, the limited shared beta account is used. Say “show my quota”; personal quotas are per user per server, and rate/concurrency caps apply even with your own key. Shared-account requests also draw from that server's shared pool. A connection is not a Pro subscription.

/connect start creates a private five-minute, single-use form link. Local onboarding is currently operator-only: it must be opened on the bot's Mac. Friends cannot use this loopback form remotely; public HTTPS onboarding is a release gate. Supported connections are dedicated Groq or OpenRouter API keys. OpenRouter connections use its free-model router. Provider availability, account limits, and billing remain your responsibility.

Never paste keys, Discord tokens, passwords, browser cookies, or payment details in chat. The bot rejects recognizable key/token patterns but cannot identify every secret. Use provider-side budget controls where available. Consumer chat subscriptions are not treated as API access.

Saved keys are authenticated-encrypted and bound to your Discord account across servers. Admins cannot borrow your key. /connect disconnect removes the live saved connection everywhere; revoke the key at the provider to invalidate all copies. Recovery backups can retain encrypted old records.

When something doesn't work

  • No reply? Check that the bot is online, you invoked it, and the channel is enabled. Try /help.
  • Quota or busy notice? Wait for the displayed reset/retry time. Do not create more keys to evade provider limits.
  • Action denied? You and the bot need the relevant permissions; the role must be below both hierarchies and not privileged/managed.
  • Summary empty? Source messages must be readable. Private source channels cannot be summarized into a different public channel.
  • Manager setup? Use /setup, /config, and /plan status. Don't grant Administrator just to bypass a warning.

Your data and feedback

/data status shows saved record counts, /data export exports your server-scoped records without credentials, and /data delete requests confirmation before deleting them. Data deletion also removes your global personal provider connection; shared server workflows remain. Backups require separate deletion through support. Use /feedback for beta feedback or the support tracker, without secrets or private conversation text. Read the privacy policy and terms.
