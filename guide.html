Inside a server where Sparuko is installed, mention it and say what you need. Try @Sparuko starter pack. You can also reply to a bot message, type Sparuko, before your request, or use the configured prefix (default !ai). A manager can change that prefix through /config.

Sparuko is the only supported invocation name. Old name aliases no longer respond. Your existing data, quotas and permissions are unchanged. A bare mention gets a short invitation; ask for help when you want the full menu.

Availability: invite-only beta, currently hosted on the operator's Mac. No payments or uptime guarantee. This website is a guide, not the private API-key portal.

You don't need to memorize a command format. Informal requests and common typos can be interpreted for supported utilities, such as “pls make us a poll asking Chess or Minecraft” or “remid me to drink water in 2 hours”. If details are ambiguous, Sparuko asks instead of guessing. Interpretation uses AI quota; channel summarization after interpretation can use an additional request. Exact local examples below avoid that interpretation cost. This is a bounded supported-tool system, not a guarantee to understand or execute every possible sentence.

Playing and exact local invocations use no AI quota; loosely worded requests may use an AI interpretation request first.

Actions, safely

Try “create a channel called roadmap”, “roadmap naam ka channel bana do”, “set slowmode in this channel to 30 seconds”, or “assign the Member role to @person”. You must already have the relevant Discord permission. Sparuko proposes only supported actions, then asks you to confirm. It checks current permissions, bot permissions, and role hierarchy again before acting. Confirmations expire after five minutes.

@Sparuko @person ko ping kar proposes one human-member ping in the current channel. Use a real Discord mention, not a bare name or “him”. Only the requester can confirm; the target must still have access. One ping per sender per minute and per target every five minutes across the server. No DM, mass ping, or forced online status. Notification settings still apply.

No auto-muting, live web/news/weather feeds, category creation, unrestricted shell, remote browser, arbitrary MCP tools, role privilege escalation, or autonomous destructive actions. A prompt cannot grant permissions. Use /access for capabilities and /action history for recent action records.

Opt-in memory and automation

“Remember this conversation” enables your channel-scoped context for 24 hours. Responses remain visible to channel readers. “Forget this conversation” clears it. Disabling use alone does not delete saved context. An explicit reply can include one recent (within 15 minutes) referenced bot text answer as unsaved chat context, with fresh read-permission checks. It does not scan ambient history, enable memory, select action targets, or grant confirmation.

Server Pro managers can say “watch this channel and post a daily digest”. A public channel notice appears first; each run uses at most 100 recent human messages and the automation expires in 30 days. Say “show digests”, “run the digest now”, or “stop the digest”. This is periodic summarization, not continuous autonomous monitoring. Digests currently use the shared beta account, never personal connected keys.

Accounts, keys, and quota

Say “my connection” to see which account is used. Interactive AI requests use your connected key when present; failures do not silently switch to the operator's account. Without a connection, the limited shared beta account is used. Say “show my quota”; personal quotas are per user per server, and rate/concurrency caps apply even with your own key. Shared-account requests also draw from that server's shared pool. A connection is not a Pro subscription.

/connect start creates a private five-minute, single-use form link. Local onboarding is currently operator-only: it must be opened on the bot's Mac. Friends cannot use this loopback form remotely; public HTTPS onboarding is a release gate. Supported connections are dedicated Groq or OpenRouter API keys. OpenRouter connections use its free-model router. Provider availability, account limits, and billing remain your responsibility.

Never paste keys, Discord tokens, passwords, browser cookies, or payment details in chat. The bot rejects recognizable key/token patterns but cannot identify every secret. Use provider-side budget controls where available. Consumer chat subscriptions are not treated as API access.

Saved keys are authenticated-encrypted and bound to your Discord account across servers. Admins cannot borrow your key. /connect disconnect removes the live saved connection everywhere; revoke the key at the provider to invalidate all copies. Recovery backups can retain encrypted old records.

When something doesn't work

  • No reply? Check that the bot is online, you invoked it, and the channel is enabled. Try /help.
  • Quota or busy notice? Wait for the displayed reset/retry time. Do not create more keys to evade provider limits.
  • Action denied? You and the bot need the relevant permissions. Roles must be below both hierarchies, not managed, and cannot grant permissions either you or the bot lacks.
  • Summary empty? Source messages must be readable. Private source channels cannot be summarized into a different public channel.
  • Manager setup? Use /setup, /config, and /plan status. Don't grant Administrator just to bypass a warning.

Your data and feedback

/data status shows saved record counts, /data export exports your server-scoped records without credentials, and /data delete requests confirmation before deleting them. Data deletion also removes your global personal provider connection; shared server workflows remain. Minimal daily quota counters expire separately (current/previous UTC day, hourly cleanup while online); deleting content cannot reset your allowance. The shared beta also has an operator-wide attempt cap across all servers and digests. Backups require separate deletion through support. Use /feedback for beta feedback or the support tracker, without secrets or private conversation text. Read the privacy policy and terms.
