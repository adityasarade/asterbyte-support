Useful in chat

Operational

Create on-demand or consent-visible scheduled channel summaries, reusable workflows, reminders, threads, channels, role changes, and announcements.

Permission-aware

Discord permissions are checked before proposing an action and checked again immediately before execution.

Tenant-isolated

Quotas, memory, workflows, configuration, and audit records are scoped by Discord server and user.
