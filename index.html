The AsterByte personality

Warm voice. Clear boundaries.

A bit curious, a bit playful, and practical first. No fake “done!” messages, no pretending to be human, and no jokes when you need a serious answer.

Your provider. Clear limits.

Bring your brain.
We'll bring the interface.

The beta has a limited shared Groq account. Personal Groq and OpenRouter keys are supported with encrypted storage and no silent account fallback. Self-service connection currently works only for the operator on the bot's Mac; public HTTPS onboarding isn't deployed yet.

Official APIs only—not consumer chat logins, cookies or subscription workarounds. Provider limits and bot quotas apply. Exact local games use no AI; interpreting loose wording may consume a request.
